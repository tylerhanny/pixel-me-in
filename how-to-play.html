How to play

You run by yourself. Jump over things on the ground, slide under things in the air, and grab every stick of RAM before the data center does. Every 180 m you are zapped to a new place.

Controls

Touch screen

Jump
Tap anywhere (or swipe up). Tap again in the air to double jump.
Slide
Swipe down and hold. In the air, this drops you down fast.
Pause
The pause button in the top right corner.

One hand is enough – play upright or sideways. Prefer buttons? Turn on Jump/Slide buttons in the pause menu or in Settings.

Keyboard

Jump
Space, ↑ or W (or click). Press again in the air to double jump. Let go early for a small hop.
Slide
Hold ↓ or S. In the air, this drops you down fast.
Pause
Esc or P.

Gamepad

Jump
A or d-pad up.
Slide
B or d-pad down.
Pause
Start.

Power-ups

The first one shows up at about 120 m, then at least one every 250 m.

Firewall
Blocks one crash.
Download More RAM
Pulls nearby RAM to you, and every stick counts double, for 8 seconds.
Jetpack
Fly over everything for 5 seconds.

RAM

A normal stick is 1 GB. Now and then a rare RGB stick shows up: 8 GB. Hit 8, 16, 32 or 64 GB in one run and see what happens.

The Utilizer

Your desk, then the data center (more places are coming). Near the end of each place The Utilizer attacks: slide under his beam, jump over his cloud drops. If you trip, he runs right behind you and grabs a quarter of your RAM – trip again while he is close and he gets you.

First run

Your first run teaches you the moves. Time slows down until you do each one: jump, slide, double jump, grab a power-up. You can replay it from How to play in the game.

Unlocks

Long runs and saved RAM unlock new looks in the Photo Booth:

  • Crown and space helmet: run 1,500 m in one go.
  • 3D glasses: save 150 GB of RAM in total.
  • Space suit: run 5,000 m in total.
  • Lab coat: play 10 runs.

Tips

  • Press jump a moment before you land – it still counts.
  • Hold slide in the air to drop fast and slide right away.
  • Slow mode and reduce motion are in Settings.
